FILE: 01_RAD_DINO_Finetuning_and_Fusion.ipynb
WHAT THE NOTEBOOK DOES
1. Installs/imports the libraries and fixes random seeds.
2. Loads 61,471 pre-generated ROI crops and metadata (44,093 train, 8,713 validation, 8,665 test).
3. Checks that patient IDs do not overlap between train, validation and test.
4. Resizes while preserving aspect ratio, pads to 224x224, normalizes using RAD-DINO preprocessing, and applies mild affine/brightness augmentation.
5. Loads microsoft/rad-dino and fine-tunes the last six transformer blocks plus the classification head.
6. Uses early stopping based on validation average precision.
7. Combines detector confidence with RAD-DINO probability, and also learns a logistic-regression score fusion.
8. Selects thresholds using validation data to retain at least 74% of positive proposals, then evaluates on test.
9. Plots training/precision-recall curves.

RESULTS RECORDED IN THE NOTEBOOK
- Trainable parameters: 42.93M out of 86.98M.
- Training stopped early at epoch 5 (no validation AP improvement for 3 epochs).
- Best validation: AUROC 0.7607, AP 0.5877.
- Test: AUROC 0.7881, AP 0.6113.
- Detector confidence baseline: 63.86% FP reduction, 77.92% TP retention, AUC 0.7855, AP 0.6015.
- Confidence × RAD-DINO: 64.44% FP reduction, 79.83% TP retention, AUC 0.8057, AP 0.6343.
- Learned logistic-regression fusion: 66.07% FP reduction, 79.28% TP retention, AUC 0.8076, AP 0.6377.
- At fixed threshold 0.1, confidence × RAD-DINO gave 72.36% FP reduction and 74.14% TP retention.
